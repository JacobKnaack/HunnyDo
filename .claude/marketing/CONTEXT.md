# HunnyDo Marketing Context

Every marketing agent reads this file before doing anything. Keep it current: it's
the single source of truth for positioning, audience and voice.

## What HunnyDo is

A client document and action chasing service. A firm creates a dedicated digital
checklist for each client (files to upload, questions to answer, items to confirm).
HunnyDo handles the nagging, collecting and organizing in the background, so nobody
has to dig through email threads or keep manual checklists.

**One-liner:** Stop chasing clients for documents. HunnyDo does the nagging for
you.

**Core product objects (use these words):**

- **Checklist**: one per client or engagement
- **Item types**: Upload (file requested), Answer (text / URL / email), Check off
(one-tap confirmation)
- **Reminders**: automatic follow-ups until items are done
- **Saved checklists**: reusable templates (e.g. "Tax return intake", "Mortgage
pre-approval docs")
- Plans: Free and Paid (paid unlocks saved contacts, existing-client picker, more).
Check /pricing for current numbers. Never quote prices from memory.

## Who we sell to (ICP)

Small to mid-size firms (1-50 staff) whose work stalls while they wait on clients.

| Vertical | What they chase | Pain in their words | Peak season / trigger |
| --- |--- |--- |--- |
| Accountants & bookkeepers | W-2s, 1099s, bank statements, receipts, signed engagement letters | "Half of tax season is emailing clients for the same PDFs" | Jan-Apr, Sept-Oct extensions, month-end close |
| Mortgage brokers / loan officers | Pay stubs, 2 yrs tax returns, bank statements, ID, gift letters | "Deals slip because a borrower forgot one statement" | Rate drops, spring buying season |
| Digital agencies | Brand assets, logins, copy, content approvals, access to GA / ad accounts | "Kickoff waits 3 weeks on logins" | New client onboarding |
| Legal assistants / paralegals | Intake forms, IDs, signed retainers, evidence, discovery docs | "We chase the same intake packet every matter" | New matters, discovery deadlines |

**Buyer:** owner / partner / ops manager. **User:** admin, paralegal, account manager, processor.
**Alternatives we replace:** email threads, spreadsheets, shared Drive folders, generic portals (e.g. client portals bundled in practice management tools), Content Snare, FileInvite, Clustdoc and similar. The researcher agent keeps the competitor list current in `marketing/research/competitors.md`.

## Voice

From the product design system. It applies to marketing too:
- **Name the action, not the mechanism.** "Send the checklist," not "Leverage our platform."
- **Plain, warm, a little playful.** The playfulness comes from the honey/hive theme and light wit, never from exclamation marks or hype.
- **Say what happens and what to do next.** No apologizing, no blaming the client or the reader.
- **Concrete over abstract.** "Your client gets a link, uploads the pay stub from their phone, done" beats "streamlined document workflows."
- Banned: "revolutionize", "game-changer", "seamless", "unlock", "leverage", "synergy", "in today's fast-paced world", emoji walls.
- Brand name is always **HunnyDo** (capital H, capital D, one word).

## Hard rules for all agents

1. **Drafts only.** Agents never publish, post, send or schedule anything. A human reviews and ships.
2. **No invented proof.** No fake testimonials, customer names, quotes, logos or statistics. Use `[PLACEHOLDER: real stat/customer]` where proof is needed. Third-party stats need a source link.
3. **Compliance:** cold email must be CAN-SPAM / GDPR-aware (real sender, physical address, opt-out line). Never claim legal, tax or lending advice. Never imply security certifications we don't hold.
4. **Community rules first.** On Reddit, forums or Facebook groups, follow each community's self-promotion rules. Default to helpful answers with no link.
5. Save output to `marketing/` paths named in each agent. Don't overwrite. Date-stamp new files `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`.
