## What the app does

HunnyDo is a web application that creates checklist that are to be completed by
an assignee. When the user signs up they can create contacts (email and name),
and create a checklist of tasks.  When the the checklist is complete, and notification
is sent to the assignee with a link to checklist page.  When tasks are completed,
a notification is sent back the checklist owner that someone has completed their
tasks.

## Audience

This app is marketed to both everyday individuals as well as professionals.  The
target audience are Freelancers, Marketing Agencies, Mortgage Brokers, and Law
Firms that need to reach out to client to get deliverables completed in a timely
manner.
